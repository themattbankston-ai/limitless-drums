# Sample build wrap-up, 11 November 2026

What was open on 10 November and where it stands. "Verified" means a test in `tests/` checks it and passes on
Linux; "not verified" means nothing has measured or heard it yet.

## Done today

### Projects (save and recall)
- **Save** (top bar, or Cmd/Ctrl+S) writes `<take>.ldproj` beside the recordings: every setting, each mic's
  alignment as applied, track names, the monitor mix, the reference's identity. No audio.
- Opening the folder, the recordings or the `.ldproj` recalls it. The hits come back exactly as saved; Auto Prep
  runs once to rebuild its measurements, then each mic's saved delay, polarity and phase EQ are put back as they
  were, whatever today's measurement says. A project saved by a newer version, with a recording missing, or whose
  recordings changed is refused with the reason.
- A dot on Save and "(edited)" in the title show unsaved changes; opening another take or quitting asks first.
- The room-mic choice is kept between launches (app preference).
- Verified: recall exports the same bytes as before saving (tone, clean-up, nudge, flip, hand-set phase EQ band,
  tuning, level curve, bit depth, a renamed track, monitor mix); a saved alignment that differs from today's
  measurement comes back as saved; a failed save leaves the old project whole; a moved folder is found.
- Not saved: undo history, the view (zoom, selection, step), the reference's audio (its file path is; the match
  itself is saved in full).
- Limit: tuning ratios are measured again on recall. If they differ by more than half a cent the app says so.

### Export cancel
- While it writes, the Export button reads "Cancel · NN %" and Esc cancels too; everything else is locked.
- Cancel is honoured between renders, up to the moment the files are written. Verified: a cancelled export writes
  nothing, leaves files already in the folder alone, puts the monitor mix back, and the next export is
  byte-identical to an uninterrupted one.
- Limit: once the files start being written (the last second or two) it finishes.

### Track names
- Double-click a name in any track header to change it. The new name is shown everywhere and is what `{MIC}`
  writes. How the mic is handled (room, bottom and so on) still goes by the recording. Two tracks can't share a
  name. Undo works. Verified.
- Typing takes letters, digits, space, `-`, `_`, `.`, `(`, `)`.

### Tune details
- **Details** on Tune adds, under The drum: the selected hit's waveform on the lead with the stretch the pitch was
  read from shaded, and a row per hit in the view: read (Hz), clarity (0-100, how far the pitch's peak stands
  over the rest), how far it moves, where it lands. Read-only; scope and processing don't change.
- The analysis window is shown, not adjustable (the handoff lists adjusting it as a proposal).
- Not verified by a test (drawing only); checked by capture.

### Reference: listen to it, and the remaining review items
- **Hear it** (Tone) plays the reference in place of the take at the hit you're on, at the take's loudness when
  Level match is on. A and B dim and "REFERENCE PLAYING" shows in the audition bar; A, B, the button again, export
  or removing the reference go back. Verified: neither A nor B plays, mutes and solos are untouched, export turns
  it off and writes the take.
- **Stale match**: if hits, alignment, clean-up or tuning change after the reference was measured, Tone says the
  match was measured before the take last changed. Undoing the change clears it. Verified.
- **RM02 (your side)**: engine 2 uses every round robin of the hardest layer, up to twelve, median per band.
  Verified: one badly dulled round robin of five moves the match by 0.6 dB at most; one dulled hit of five in the
  reference by under 0.5 dB. Policy, stated: a reference played softer than your hardest layer is still compared
  with the hardest layer.
- **RM05**: what is measured on your side is now written down in `src/hl/Prep.h`. Two engine 2 additions:
  where your own mics cancel in the sum (6 dB or more under their powers added up) the band is left at 0 dB and
  reported, instead of being boosted; and a tuned take is matched for where its bands land after tuning. Verified
  on a two-mic comb and on a take tuned four semitones.
- **RM06**: verified end to end: amount at 0 exports the same bytes as no reference; undoing the load exports the
  same bytes as never loading; a recalled project has the same curve to the last digit and the same bytes, and
  the match survives the reference file being deleted (only listening needs the file).
- Engine 1 is unchanged (the main app and its pinned test).

### Found and fixed on the way
- Level gains depended on edit history by about 0.0000002 (-134 dB): they are measured on audio with the phase EQ
  in, and that audio wasn't re-rendered when only a band moved. It is now, so the same settings always export the
  same bytes.
