## GUI foundation
- `Style.h` now holds the graphite/coral tokens (`tok::`): surfaces, text, coral (selection and the primary action),
  mint (on/play), states, spacing, type and control sizes, with contrast ratios measured by the WCAG formula
  (not a certification). The steps' existing colour names map onto the tokens, so every screen follows them.
- Primitives: primary button, segmented choice, switch, knob (arcs calibrated against a render: Visage measures
  from 3 o'clock and takes half the span), panel, focus ring. `LD_GALLERY=1` draws them all for review.
- Applied to the shell: coral current step, coral A/B and audition mode, mint play/counter/toggles.
- Not yet: step screens still use their own sliders and layouts (Align, Tone, Clean redesigns are Milestones 3–5).
